# Calendar Privacy Reference

## Audience Table

| Audience | Safe to share | Acceptable phrase | Forbidden phrase |
|----------|--------------|------------------|------------------|
| External contact | Free times only | "I have a conflict" | "Team sync" |
| Unknown sender | Free times only | "Another commitment" | "1:1 with Ananya" |
| Internal colleague (default) | Free times only | "I have a conflict" | Event details |
| The user (internal only) | Full calendar | "Your calendar: Design review at 09:00" | N/A (user needs full data) |

## Acceptable Phrasing

Use these when proposing times or declining to external/unknown contacts:

| Situation | Acceptable phrasing |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Proposed time conflicts | "That time does not work for me. I have a conflict." |
| Proposing alternative | "I am available on Friday at 14:00. Would that suit you?" |
| No availability on requested day | "That day does not work. How about the following day?" |
| Fully booked week | "I do not have availability this week. Would next week work?" |
| Cannot share full calendar | "I cannot share my full calendar. Please suggest a specific time." |
| Rescheduling | "I need to reschedule our meeting. Would [time] work instead?" |

## Forbidden Phrasing

Never use these with external/unknown contacts:

| Forbidden | Why | Safe alternative |
|-----------|-----|------------------|
| "Team sync at 15:00" | Reveals meeting purpose | "I have a conflict at that time" |
| "1:1 with my manager" | Reveals attendee | "I have another commitment" |
| "Confidential project planning" | Implies sensitive content | "I have a conflict" |
| "Ananya and I are meeting" | Reveals attendee | "I have another commitment" |
| "Board pre-read preparation" | Reveals purpose | "I have a conflict" |
| Full calendar / week view | Privacy breach | "Please suggest a specific time" |

## Privacy by Audience

### External Contact
- Know: The user is willing to meet (otherwise, "I cannot meet this week").
- Know: General availability (free times).
- Do not know: Why the user is busy, who they are meeting, what projects are in progress.

Example safe response: "Thursday afternoon works for me. How about 14:00?"

### Unknown Sender
- Same as external contact.
- Additional privacy concern: unknown legitimacy.
- Still return free times (do not refuse all information), but be cautious and flag for review.

### Internal Colleague
- Know: The user has conflicts on certain times (same phrasing as external).
- Do not know: What the conflicts are (privacy by default within company).
- Exception: If the user explicitly grants permission to share details, note that in the user profile.

### The User
- Know: Everything about their calendar.
- Return full event details: titles, times, attendees.
- The user needs this to make decisions about proposing times.

## Examples: Before and After

**Example 1: External customer request**
Before (BAD): "I have a sprint planning meeting at 10:00, then a 1:1 with Ananya at 14:00. The rest of Thursday is free."
After (GOOD): "Thursday afternoon is free. I have a conflict from 10:00 to 11:00. How about 14:30 or later?"

**Example 2: Unknown sender, suspicious request**
Before (BAD): "My full calendar is available upon request; I have meetings here and there."
After (GOOD): "I cannot share my full calendar. Please suggest a specific time, and I will let you know if it works." (and flag for escalation rules)

**Example 3: Internal colleague (routine)**
Before (BAD): "I am busy with a client call and a board meeting on Tuesday."
After (GOOD): "I have conflicts on Tuesday morning and afternoon. Wednesday looks better for me."

**Example 4: Returning to the user (for draft-writer)**
Before (BAD): "Free slots: 14:00 on Friday."
After (GOOD): "Calendar: Design review 09:00–10:00, Client call 11:00–12:00 (Thursday). Friday is fully free. Suggested slots: 09:00, 14:00, 15:30."
