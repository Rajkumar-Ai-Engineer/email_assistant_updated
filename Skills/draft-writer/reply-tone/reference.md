# Reply Tone Reference

## Tone and Length Guide

| Situation | Tone | Length | Opening | Closing |
|-----------|------|--------|---------|---------|
| Internal scheduling (routine) | Warm, direct | 20–50 words | "Thanks for reaching out" | "Best, Priya" |
| Manager request | Polished, acknowledging | 50–100 words | "I appreciate this" | "Regards, Priya" |
| External inquiry | Polished, brief | 40–80 words | "Thank you for reaching out" | "Best regards, Priya" |
| Angry customer | Calm, professional | 60–150 words | "I sincerely apologize" | "Regards, Priya" |
| Decline | Polite, offering alternative | 30–60 words | "Thank you for your interest" | "Best, Priya" |
| Clarification request | Friendly | 30–50 words | "Could you clarify" | "Thanks, Priya" |

## Phrase Bank

### Openings
- "Thank you for reaching out."
- "I appreciate this request."
- "Thanks for the update."
- "I understand your concern."
- "I sincerely apologize for [specific issue]."

### Acknowledgements
- "I see that you are asking about [topic]."
- "I understand the deadline is [time]."
- "I recognize this is urgent."
- "Thank you for flagging this."

### Substance: Answering
- "The answer is [fact]. This is documented in [evidence]."
- "I have found the [document] you requested."
- "I am available [time options]."

### Substance: Holding or Escalating
- "I am looking into this and will get back to you [by time]."
- "I am escalating this to [team] for immediate attention."
- "Let me review and confirm."

### Substance: Declining
- "Unfortunately, I do not have availability at that time."
- "That date does not work for me. Would [alternative] be possible?"
- "I am unable to [action], but I can [alternative]."

### Next Steps
- "Let me know if you have any questions."
- "Looking forward to hearing from you."
- "I will follow up shortly."
- "Please confirm, and I will proceed."

### Sign-offs
- "Best regards, Priya" (formal, external)
- "Best, Priya" (standard)
- "Regards, Priya" (neutral, professional)
- "Thanks, Priya" (warm, internal)

## Things Never to Say

| Forbidden statement | Why | Safe alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "I approve this" | Agent cannot decide | "I will review and get back to you" |
| "This is confirmed" | Agent cannot book events | "Would this time work for you?" |
| "I will refund X" | Agent has no authority | "I am escalating to Finance for review" |
| "Our product supports [feature]" | Agent has no product knowledge | "I will check with the team and confirm" |
| "You should do X" | Agent should not advise | "Here is what you could consider: [options]" |
| "Don't worry, it is fine" | Agent cannot assess | "I am looking into this" |
| "I remember [personal detail]" | Agent has no memory | "As stated in your email..." |
